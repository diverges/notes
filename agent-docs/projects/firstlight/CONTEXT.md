## 2026-10-02: Water washout cause isolated

Further investigation resolves the prior incomplete diagnosis: FlatKit Water additional-light stage washes out the water when runtime Moon fill (secondary directional) is enabled, even at measured intensity0.006. Held game camera at a shoreline; diagnostic shader output proved depth gradient/opaque sampling and pre-lighting color/foam correct. Original shader with Moon fill disabled recovered teal water, transparency and shoreline foam with Deferred, CRT and batching enabled; re-enabling only Moon fill reproduced pale sheet. Vendor AdditionalLights sums attenuation separately then lerps lightColor from white by Light Color Contribution (0.2), adds that independently of weak light intensity. Recommend correcting this shader contribution while preserving moon lighting elsewhere; no production fix requested/applied. Restored camera rig/brain, Moon fill, original Ocean material, Deferred mode2, CRT enabled and batching enabled; exited Play Mode and removed temporary diagnostic shader. No scene/production asset save. GEN_water-no-moon.png and GEN_water-moon-restored.png under Docs/Images show the decisive comparison. Supersedes earlier foam/depth suspicion.
